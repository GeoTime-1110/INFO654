The Farm in Iowa

this text was ChatGPT generated with the prompt "create a seven stanza poem about Iowa."

I. Morning Light

The sun climbs up o’er fields of gold, And wakes the earth from nights of cold. The farmhouse stands beneath the sky, While geese and sparrows flutter by. The rooster calls his bright “cock-a-doodle,” As dew shines fresh on grass and poodle.

II. Fields of Corn

The rows of corn stretch far and wide, Like emerald waves at summer’s tide. The gentle wind moves leaf by leaf, And whispers songs of sweet relief. The tassels dance in morning air, While sunlight paints the landscape fair.

III. The Barn

The old red barn stands proud and tall, With weathered boards and shadows on the wall. Inside, the hay smells warm and sweet, Where horses rest on tired feet. The cows stand chewing, calm and slow, As dusty beams let sunlight glow.

IV. The Farmer’s Day

Before the dawn, the farmer rises, To meet the day and its surprises. With muddy boots and hands of toil, He tends the animals and soil. Through summer heat and winter rain, He works the fields again, again.

V. Summer Storm

Dark clouds roll in across the plain, And patter softly turns to rain. The thunder rumbles, deep and low, As silver streams through furrows flow. Then sunlight breaks through clouds above, And leaves the world washed clean with love.

VI. Harvest Time

The golden fields are ripe and bright, The combines hum from morn till night. The grain bins fill, the wagons roll, A harvest song warms every soul. The work is hard, but hearts are glad, For all the gifts the earth has had.

VII. Iowa Dreams

When evening paints the sky in red, And stars appear above the shed, The crickets sing their lullaby, As moonlight rests on fields nearby. This farm, where simple joys remain, Is home through sunshine, snow, and rain. And in Iowa’s wide embrace, My heart has found its resting place.
